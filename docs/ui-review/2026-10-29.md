# Review of the workbench, 2026-10-29

The first review of the workbench by the interface designer (W10; proposal 0015, step 14), against
[UI_GUIDE.md](../UI_GUIDE.md). Built from main at 3439623 with `npm run build` and `node scripts/preview.mjs`, opened
in Chromium at 375 px and 1280 px wide, light and dark, with an example loaded where the workspace has one (ethanol +
water T-x-y; methanol + acetone + chloroform flash; examples/heater-flash.chepta.json in the flowsheet). 44
screenshots were taken; the ones the findings point to are in [2026-10-29/](2026-10-29/).

What works well: the layout (ribbon, Inputs, canvas, Results) is the same in every workspace; light and dark both
read well, with no unreadable token found; the page never scrolls sideways at 375 px; locked models say "not
available yet"; the empty states say what to do.

## Fixed in this pull request (plainly broken, small, in W10's files)

- A. `src/ui/icons.js` defined the warning icon twice (esbuild's duplicate key "warn"): one removed.
- B. Phones: the Flowsheet ribbon cut its block buttons off at the right ("Heater/Co", "Heat exch"; screenshot 11).
  The two-row button groups now wrap into two columns (screenshot 15).
- C. Charts: the label "Pressures, kPa" was hidden under its input (screenshot 04: "Pre kPa"). A field whose input
  takes the whole width now puts the label above it (screenshot 16).

## To choose from

Each item: what is wrong, why it matters, the fix proposed, and its size. Pick the numbers to fix; W10 fixes them in
small pull requests of their own (the ones in other workstreams' views with their owner).

1. **Chart text too small on phones.** At 375 px the T-x-y axis labels are about 6 px high (screenshot 03): the chart
   is drawn at desktop size and shrunk. *Matters:* the main result cannot be read on a phone. *Fix:* draw the chart
   with the width it is shown at (or scale the font up when narrow), so text stays at 11 px or more. *Medium*
   (plot.js, txy.js and the other charts).
2. **A large empty gap under the chart on phones.** Between the legend and "Download Excel" there are about 150 px of
   nothing (screenshot 03), and in the flowsheet a tall empty drawing area (screenshot 11). *Matters:* the results
   look missing; the user scrolls past blank space. *Fix:* no fixed minimum height for the canvas when narrow. *Small*.
3. **Round-off shown as numbers.** The flowsheet shows "Heat out 7.36e-9 kW", "Energy balance 6e-14 kW" and
   "Q-V1 −7.4e-9 kW" for an adiabatic drum (screenshot 10). *Matters:* an engineer reads it as a small duty, or as a
   bug. *Fix:* show values below the solver's tolerance as 0 (and the balance as "closed"). *Small* (flowsheet view).
4. **Overlapping labels on drawings and charts.** In the flowsheet, the stream name "S4" sits on the port label
   "liquid" (screenshot 10); on the T-s chart the isobar labels "10000 kPa" and "1000 kPa" crowd each other at the top
   (screenshot 04). *Matters:* hard to read, looks unfinished. *Fix:* move a stream name off the port label; stagger
   or move end labels that would overlap. *Small to medium*.
5. **Numbers formatted differently in one table.** In the flash table: "2 050" and "−29 866" with a space, but
   "−13908" in Results; "6983.29" next to "3589.2"; "0.33333" in the table, "0.333333" in Inputs (screenshot 06).
   *Matters:* a column cannot be scanned, and the space reads as two numbers. *Fix:* one number format for tables: the
   same decimals down a column, no thousands space (or always one). *Small* (a shared format helper).
6. **Wide tables cut on phones without a cue.** The flash and flowsheet stream tables show two of four columns at
   375 px (screenshots 07, 11); they scroll sideways, but nothing says so. *Matters:* the vapour and liquid columns
   look missing. *Fix:* a fade at the cut edge, or the first column fixed while the others scroll. *Small*.
7. **The flowsheet drawing is cut on phones.** At 375 px the flash drum and products are outside the visible part of
   the drawing (screenshot 11); the user must pan to find them. *Fix:* fit the drawing to the width when it is first
   shown (the zoom button does it). *Small*.
8. **"Heat duty from a feed at" ends mid-sentence.** The Feed state checkbox of the flash (screenshots 06, 07) has a
   label that stops before what it refers to. *Fix:* complete the label ("Heat duty from a feed at its own T and P",
   or what was meant). *Small* (flash inputs).
9. **Code names in what the user reads.** The properties page says "h_J_mol is relative to the ideal gas at
   298.15 K; s_J_molK keeps the IF97 reference" (screenshot 05). *Matters:* the guide asks for the engineer's words.
   *Fix:* "Enthalpy h is relative to ...; entropy s keeps ...". *Small*.
10. **Parameter-set menus cut their text.** "fitted-kamihama2012 (default) · fitte" (screenshots 01, 06): the set's
    name is cut in the 227 px panel. *Matters:* the tier at the end is what the user looks for. *Fix:* show the tier
    as the badge it already has, and the menu with the source name only ("Kamihama 2012 (default)"). *Small*.
11. **The same explanation twice.** "Temperature at which the feed is that fraction vapour: VF = 0 is the bubble
    point ..." is in Inputs and again in Results (screenshot 06). *Fix:* keep it once, in Inputs. *Small*.
12. **Downloads in two places.** The flash has "Download CSV / Copy CSV" under the table and "Download Excel" far
    below (screenshot 06); the flowsheet has them in one row. *Fix:* one row of downloads at the end of the canvas,
    in every workspace (the guide's pattern). *Small*.
13. **Empty Results panels.** Before a calculation the Results panel is blank (screenshots 08, 09), while the canvas
    gives the next step. *Matters:* a third of the screen shows nothing. *Fix:* a muted line, "Results appear here
    once the inputs are complete", or hide the panel until then. *Small*.
14. **Literal colours instead of tokens.** The flowsheet's warning dot (`#d9822b`), and broken streams (`#c0392b`)
    use fixed colours, the same in both themes. They read in dark mode today, but are
    outside the token system of the guide. *Fix:* tokens `--fa-warn-mark` and `--fa-bad` with dark values. *Small*
    (W10's files).
15. **The status bar wraps on phones.** "CHEPTA 0.4.0" falls on a line of its own at 375 px (screenshot 07). *Fix:*
    hide the version when narrow, as the units cell already is. *Small* (W10's files).
16. **A lock with no name.** The Reactors group of the flowsheet ribbon shows a lone lock icon with no label
    (screenshots 10, 11, 15); the user cannot tell what is locked. *Fix:* the label of the locked block beside the
    lock, as the workspace tabs do ("Distillation, not available yet"). *Small*.
17. **The settings drawer on phones leaves a grey half-screen.** Below the drawer's content, the workbench shows
    through the scrim (screenshot 14). *Fix:* the drawer takes the full height when narrow. *Small* (W10's files).
