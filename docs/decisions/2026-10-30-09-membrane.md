# 2026-10-30-09: Proposal 0015, M3, step 9 (W3, W4): the Membrane block, with the area for a target

The coordinating session, for the maintainer: build the Membrane block of proposal 0015, Part I, section 4 (accepted
2026-10-29): solution-diffusion, each component's flux its permeance times its partial-pressure difference, in co- or
counter-current cells (Aziaba et al. 2022, open copy only); the permeate and the retentate for an area, or the area for
a target (stage cut or recovery), as the sizing (contract 4). Permeances are user inputs with a source. Written against
contracts v1. Model page: [docs/models/membrane.md](../models/membrane.md).

## What was done

- **Engine** (`src/units/membrane.js`): N well-mixed cells; in co-current flow the permeate gathered in cell k is that
  of cells 1..k, in counter-current flow that of cells k..N; one cell is complete mixing. All equations solved together
  by Newton's method (analytic Jacobian), with a continuation in the area. With `stage_cut` or `recovery` the area is
  found by Brent's method on ln A. Isothermal; the feed side at the feed's pressure, the permeate side at the given
  pressure; Dalton's partial pressures.
- **Sizing** (`membraneArea`, `src/sizing/sizing.js`, contract 4, no longer "not built"): the area and, with a fibre's
  inner diameter and length, the number of fibres, A = ε d π l (Aziaba et al., Equation (2)).
- **Request path:** the capability `solve_membrane` (CHEPTA.run, `chepta request`, MCP tool `solve_membrane`);
  project format 6 now also holds the membrane block (`docs/schema/project-6.json`); warning code `CONDENSATION`
  (contract 2: a block adds its own code).
- **Workbench:** a **Separator** button in the toolbar (as the Reactor button) with the flash drum, the component
  separator and the membrane (proposal 0015, section 4: "In one Separator button"); the membrane's form (permeances
  with their unit, permeate pressure, flow, cells, area or target, optional fibres) and its results (area, fibres, stage
  cut, recoveries).
- **Independent reference** `validation/python/reference_membrane.py` (scipy; unknowns the retentate flows, MINPACK's
  hybrid method), fixture `validation/fixtures/membrane.json`: the engine agrees within 1e-13.
- **Worked example:** H. Binous, Chem. Eng. Educ. 40 (2006) 140-144 (free on the journal's archive). The ternary case by
  complete mixing: all six printed figures of the area and the compositions reproduced. Oxygen from air: complete
  mixing 3.2314e8 cm² against the printed 3.228e8 (+0.1 %); 200 cells against the printed plug-flow areas:
  counter-current 2.8620e8 against 2.859e8, co-current 2.9569e8 against 2.955e8. The tolerances of the test: 0.2 % on
  the area, 5e-4 and 2e-4 on the oxygen fractions.
- **Example** `examples/membrane-co2.chepta.json`: Aziaba et al.'s test case 2 (Table 1; Sada et al.'s cellulose
  triacetate module 31): permeances 204.2, 60.2 and 13.1 × 10⁻¹⁰ mol/(s m² Pa) for CO₂, O₂ and N₂, 303 K, 15.7 bar,
  permeate 1013.25 mbar, 270 fibres of 125 µm × 63 cm (0.0668 m²); the feed of 0.01 kmol/h is the example's choice
  (stage cut 0.325). Sada et al.'s own article is paywalled and was not read.

## What was not done

- **Pervaporation** (a liquid feed): refused, `NOT_AVAILABLE`. It needs the liquid's fugacities on the feed side and
  the heat of evaporation (the feed cools); the article's pervaporation case (Table 2) gives no feed flow, so it cannot
  be reproduced as a check.
- Aziaba et al.'s gas-permeation comparisons (test cases 2 and 3) are figures only: not used as numbers. Their case 3
  permeances serve an engine-against-reference case, at an area and a feed flow chosen here.
- Pressure drop in the fibres, a sweep gas, temperature-dependent permeances, an energy balance with the permeate's
  expansion: not modelled; the block says so in its notes.

## The maintainer's answers (2026-11-04)

Relayed by the coordinating session:

1. The flash drum stays inside the Separator button.
2. 20 cells stay the default. How far they are from Binous's plug-flow answers is stated in
   [docs/models/membrane.md](../models/membrane.md) ("How far 20 cells are from plug flow"): counter-current 0.80 %
   above the printed area (2.8819e8 against 2.859e8 cm²), co-current 0.56 % above (2.9715e8 against 2.955e8 cm²).
3. Isothermal outlets with the duty reported; an adiabatic module is postponed until needed.
4. Pervaporation is postponed until needed.
5. Counter-current flow and GPU stay the defaults.
