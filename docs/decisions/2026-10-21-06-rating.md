# 2026-10-21: rating a given heat exchanger (proposal 0013, step 8)

The maintainer asked (2026-10-21): "ok, now do the last points from the proposals and then we go to the reactions". The
last open step of proposal 0013 was Rating, locked since step 2.

**What it does** (`exchangerRating` in `src/units/heat-exchange.js`). Given the area, U and the arrangement, it finds
the duty at which the zone areas of pre-sizing add up to the area given; the outlets and the effectiveness follow. The
same zone model as pre-sizing, so a rated exchanger designed back for its own outlets has its own area (within 0.3 % in
the tests). Water and a fluid with inlet and outlet give their flow instead of the outlet temperature.

**Where.** In the Heat exchanger workspace, the ribbon's Calculation group is now Design or Rating (the locked button
is gone): in rating the area replaces the process outlet or the specification, and water or a fluid gives its flow.
In the flowsheet, the `hx` block and the heater with a utility take `A_m2` (with U) as their one specification.

**Design first, then rate** (the maintainer, 2026-10-21: "should be different for the flowsheet. the area should be
calculated at the design phase as a pre-sizing and then it is kept that area and U"). In the flowsheet there is no
"Area" specification to type: a block is designed and solved, then "Rate this exchanger" keeps the area and U of that
solution (and the utility's flow of the design), and "Back to the design" restores the design. In the workspace,
switching to Rating starts from the area and the utility flow of the design just shown; the placeholder values of the
first version (10 m², 5000 kg/h) remain only for a workspace that never showed a design.

**Checked** (`test/rating.test.js`): Lienhard and Lienhard's Example 3.5 rated (30 m², U = 500 W/(m² K), parallel flow:
656.0 kW, 84.4 and 72.8 °C; the book rounds ε to 0.596 and reads 655.6 kW); every arrangement against its
effectiveness-NTU relation (Lienhard and Lienhard, Table 3.1 and eqns. 3.20-3.21) within 0.2 %; one side at a constant
temperature against 1 − exp(−NTU); process streams of ethanol and water (NRTL, ideal-gas vapour) against steam and
cooling water.
