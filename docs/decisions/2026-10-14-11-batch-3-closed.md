# 2026-10-14: batch 3 of proposal 0009 closed

- **Asked:** "for batch three leftovers lets decide on the small decisions to end this. take the best decision and
  close this."
- **2-Methylfuran:** its record kept every set within its own uncertainty and left out Tai 2014, which lies 5-6 %
  above the two other sets (Liebergesell 2015, Lomba 2015). It is now fitted by the pooling rule with Tai 2014 left
  out for a stated reason: the same article lies 4-7 % above the other measured set for furfuryl alcohol too, so its
  offset is systematic, not scatter. The two remaining sets agree (mean deviations -0.06 and -0.05 %); the normal
  boiling point, 337.01 K, meets the WebBook average of the measured values (337 +/- 1 K).
- **Isoamyl alcohol (added):** its twelve vapour-pressure sets scatter from -2.7 to +1.9 % around the curve of all of
  them, seven by more than their stated uncertainties, and no set is singled out by an independent check. Pooled, with
  the same weight per article, and the spread stated as the record's uncertainty (about 3 %); `vp_accept` stops the
  build if a set moves further. The independent checks support the pooled curve: normal boiling point 404.43 K
  against 403.85-404.90 K measured in six articles (median 404.48 K) and the WebBook average 404 +/- 2 K; the
  enthalpy of vaporization from its slope is 0.6-1.4 % below the two calorimetric values of Majer, Svoboda et al.
  (1985). The gap between its liquid densities (to 332.7 K) and vapour pressures (from 335.85 K) was accepted
  before.
- **Isoamyl alcohol's pairs:** the archive has activity-model data for ten pairs; four NRTL sets are written within the
  quality limits (1-propanol 0.13 K, 2-propanol 0.49 K, 1,4-butanediol 0.18 K, isobutanol 0.10 K and 0.005 in y). Six
  have no set meeting the rules (1-butanol, 1-hexanol, ethanol, n-butyl acetate and N-methyl-2-pyrrolidone: no
  isobaric T-x-y set and no isotherm with the pure-component pressures; DMSO: the point test fails). The archive has no
  vapour-liquid set for isoamyl alcohol + water.
- **No open measured vapour pressure:**
  - HMF, lactic acid and lactide go to the nonvolatile class (step 5 of the proposal): HMF decomposes before it
    boils, lactic acid is hardly volatile in the processes where it appears, lactide is a solid at room temperature;
  - solketal and alpha-angelica lactone are volatile liquids: treating them as nonvolatile would make every
    distillation with them wrong, so they stay out, as "no open data", until open vapour pressures are found.
- Batch 3 is closed with this.
