# 2026-11-12-04: the canvas tabs (proposal 0021, step 2)

## What was asked

Step 2 of proposal 0021 (accepted 2026-11-11; docs/decisions/2026-11-11-04-interface-reorganization.md): canvas tabs,
Plot, Table, Data and Warnings, in the phase diagrams and the Flash, then the other workspaces; the downloads move to
the Table tab. Built on the shell's contract of step 1 (`sourcesOf` for the Data tab, `dataOf`, `missingOf`). The new
layout only; the classic layout stays the default and unchanged until step 4.

The maintainer's answers to the three questions of step 1 (2026-11-12, all as recommended) are recorded in
docs/decisions/2026-11-12-01-ui-0021-shell-contract.md.

What each tab holds, in every workspace:

- **Plot** (Drawing on the flowsheet): the diagram, the drawing, or the Flash's phase split.
- **Table**: the downloads first (SVG, PNG, CSV, Excel), then the view's own table (the Flash's streams, the
  flowsheet's stream and energy tables, a column's profile), else the tables of the view's Excel export (the T-x-y's
  101 points, with units), up to 300 rows; the rest are in the Excel file. Before the inputs are complete: "No table
  until the inputs are complete".
- **Data**: what the result rests on (the pairs and their tiers, the rule of the sets: `dataOf`), the measured sets of
  a binary diagram, the note on the user's own data (proposal 0012), and the sources the calculation uses
  (`sourcesOf`), each linked, with its kind and why it is open, and a button to the Sources panel.
- **Warnings**, with a count on the tab: the known deviations and model notes of the canvas, the warnings of the
  Results panel (copied: they stay there too), the error that stopped a calculation, and the problems of incomplete
  inputs (not while nothing is chosen yet). An error (role "alert") and "Solving in the background" stay above the
  tabs, seen whatever tab is open, and are listed in Warnings as well.

No engine code, number or parameter changed. In the classic layout the canvas is the same: the classic T-x-y and Flash
screenshots of this branch and of `main` were compared pixel by pixel and differ only in the status bar's timing
("Ready, 9 ms").

## Decided (the maintainer, 2026-11-12, relayed by the coordinating session)

All four as recommended:

1. An error that stopped the calculation and "Solving in the background" stay above the tabs (and are listed in
   Warnings).
2. The Flash's Plot tab holds the phase split; its stream table is on the Table tab.
3. The Table tab shows at most 300 rows; the rest are in the Excel file.
4. The context row's Data button opens the Data tab instead of the Sources panel, in step 3 or 5 (not in this pull
   request).

One change asked in this pull request: **warnings in two steps**, "so the person is not overloaded": a short form
first, the full text only when the person presses it, one at a time or all at once; an error that stops the
calculation stays fully shown; keyboard-accessible (a button with aria-expanded); the classic layout unchanged.

Built (`twoStepWarning` and `warningList` in `src/ui/canvas-tabs.js`):

- the Warnings tab starts with the count ("2 warnings") and an **Expand all** button (aria-expanded; "Collapse all"
  when every warning is open); each warning is one line, its title or first sentence, cut with "..." where it does
  not fit, and a **Details** button (aria-expanded, aria-controls) that opens its whole text ("Less" closes it);
- the error that stopped the calculation is listed open; an error above the tabs (role "alert") stays as it is,
  fully shown;
- the warnings of the Results panel take the same form in the new layout; nothing else lists warnings above the tabs
  (the known deviations and notes moved into the Warnings tab);
- a warning that is whole in one line (nothing more to say) is shown as it is, with no button;
- the classic layout keeps its warnings as they are (`warning.js` is not changed).

Screenshots: 12 (short form, 1280 px, light), 13 (expanded with Expand all, 1280 px, dark), 14 (short form, 375 px,
light); 07 and 08 retaken in the new form.
