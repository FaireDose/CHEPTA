# 2026-10-21: the title bar never overlaps

The maintainer saw "Flowsheet" running into "Library" in the workbench's title bar on the website (2026-10-21: "make
sure that the flowsheet word doesn't overlap library"). The new logo is wider than the word it replaced, and at the
website's width (1240 px) the sections and the supporting tools no longer fit beside it.

The title bar now measures itself after every redraw and resize, and gives up space one step at a time while it
overflows (`fitTitle` in `src/ui/app.js`): less space between the buttons; then the supporting tools' labels, one at a
time from the right (each keeps its icon and its tooltip); then the short section names; then the supporting tools on a
row of their own. At 1240 px only "Feedback" becomes an icon. Checked in a browser from 1240 to 390 px wide.
