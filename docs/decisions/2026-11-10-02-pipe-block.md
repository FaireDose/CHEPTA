## What was built

- **The engine** (`src/units/pipe.js`, sessions of 2026-11-07): one fluid phase through a straight pipe, split into
  segments (20 by default). The pressure drop has four parts: friction, fittings, elevation and acceleration. The
  heat can be adiabatic, isothermal, or a loss Q = U A ΔT_lm. The outlet is a P-H flash. Every equation is cited in
  the file's header and in the proposal. A two-phase inlet, a phase change inside the pipe, a pressure that falls to
  zero and a gas that reaches choking are all refused, with the reason. It works in flowsheet files,
  `solveFlowsheet` and `runUnit`.
- **The Pipe workspace** (2026-11-10): Unit models → Pipe, between Heat exchanger and Reactor
  (`src/ui/pipe-logic.js`, `src/ui/pipe-view.js`, `pipeView` in `src/ui/app-views.js`, a pipe icon).
  - Inputs: components and the ribbon's method; the inlet (flow in kmol/h or kg/h, T, P, mole fractions); length,
    inner diameter (mm), roughness (mm) and elevation; fittings, each a row of the DOE table or your own K or Leq/D,
    with a count; heat; segments, and your own friction factor or viscosity.
  - Results: the pressure drop as the headline, then a table of friction, fittings, elevation, acceleration and total,
    each with its share. Then the outlet P and T, and the velocity, Reynolds number and friction factor at inlet and
    outlet, the regime, the density, the mass flux and the heat. The block's warnings (transition, fittings in laminar
    flow) and notes follow.
  - Canvas: the pressure along the pipe with a readout, the profile table and the Excel download.
  - Refusals are amber, with the engine's reason and what to change; they are not errors.
- **A wording fix in the engine:** the refusal for a phase change inside the pipe read "the at 5 m along the pipe is
  two-phase" and advised a flash drum. It now reads "the stream at 5 m along the pipe is two-phase" and advises raising
  the inlet pressure or moving the inlet temperature away from the boiling or dew point.
- **Screenshots** at 375 and 1280 px, light and dark, with results, with a refusal, and empty:
  `docs/ui-review/2026-11-10-pipe/`. No sideways scroll at either width.

- Two tests that list the workspaces (`test/workbench-navigation.test.js`, `test/hx-workspace.test.js`) now include
  "pipe". They count the workspaces, so a new workspace changes them (DATA_RULES section 9.2).
- **What other simulators do** (METHOD_SELECTION.md, "When in doubt"; read on 2026-11-10):
  - DWSIM's `FrictionFactor` (DWSIM.UnitOperations/FluidFlowCorrelations/FlowPackageBaseClass.vb, GPL-3.0) gives
    "If Re > 4000" an explicit formula, "ElseIf Re < 2100 Then f = 64 / Re", and between them the same Churchill
    formula as CHEPTA.
  - CHEPTA differs in two ways. It takes the limits 2000 and 3500 from the DOE handbook, an open source with a stated
    rule. It solves Colebrook exactly, the equation of the worked examples, instead of an explicit form.
  - DWSIM's pipe also has two-phase correlations (BeggsBrill.vb, LockhartMartinelli.vb). CHEPTA refuses two-phase
    flow until such a method is built from open sources (proposal 0020, step 4).
