# 2026-11-12-05: Acetic acid as a liquid below its triple point (Lab's request 3)

Accepted by the maintainer on 2026-11-12, from CHEPTA-Lab: its ethyl acetate plant feeds acetic acid at 15 degC
(288.15 K), pure and in mixtures with water and ethanol, and CHEPTA refused it (`OUT_OF_RANGE`, records from 289.81 K).

## 1. Which records stop at 289.81 K, and why

| Record | Range before | ChemSep v8.3's own range | Why it stopped at 289.81 K |
|---|---|---|---|
| liquid density | 289.81-594.45 K | 273.15-594.75 K | CHEPTA's cut: `from_chemsep` raises Tmin to the triple point |
| liquid viscosity | 289.81-474 K | 288.15-474 K | the same cut |
| surface tension | 289.81-564.72 K | 288.15-591.95 K | the same cut |
| liquid thermal conductivity | 289.81-391.05 K | 274.09-391.05 K | the same cut |
| liquid heat capacity | 289.81-400 K | **289.69**-400 K | the same cut; ChemSep itself starts at 289.69 K |
| heat of vaporization, vapour viscosity, vapour thermal conductivity | 289.81 K | 289.81 K | ChemSep's own limit (the triple point) |
| vapour pressure | 290-523.15 K | (ChemSep: 273.15 K) | the legacy record fitted to the thermo library's values; its lower end follows McDonald et al. 1959 (290.26-391 K, WebBook) |
| dimerization constant (`association`) | no range stored | | not a limit |

What blocked the flash was the **liquid heat capacity** only (`enthalpy.js`, `hLiquidPure`). A mixture's liquid
enthalpy needs each component's liquid heat capacity, so the acid in a water or ethanol mixture was refused for the same
reason. The vapour-pressure equation is evaluated **without a range check** in an activity-model system
(`system.js` `psat`; `pure().psat` does check): a flash at 288.15 K already used it, about 2 K below its stored range.

- **NIST WebBook** (pages read 2026-11-12): liquid heat capacity: Martin and Andon 1982 (T = 13-450 K, one value

  vaporization from 289 K (compilations); nothing below 287 K.
- **ThermoML Archive** (the stored retrieval of 2026-10-07; the live API answered 503 today): density from 288.15 K,
  viscosity from 293.15 K, vapour pressure from 298.15 K, liquid heat capacity only 308.15-333.15 K; no enthalpy of
  vaporization.
- **Free copies** (`open_copy.py --all`, Unpaywall, OpenAlex, CORE): none for Martin and Andon 1982
  (10.1016/0021-9614(82)90083-0), Steele et al. 1997 (10.1021/je970099y; no supplement either) or Parks and Kelley 1925
  (10.1021/ja01685a003; a 1925 article may be in a public-domain scan on HathiTrust or the Internet Archive, none found

## 3. What changed

- **Extended, ChemSep taken as it is, the limit being CHEPTA's own cut:** liquid density to 273.15 K, viscosity and
  surface tension to 288.15 K, thermal conductivity to 274.09 K, liquid heat capacity to 289.69 K. Done for acetic acid
  only (`SUPERCOOLED_CHEMSEP` in `fit_properties.py`), by running the script for it (`--only acetic-acid --write`); the
  coefficients of density and viscosity are ChemSep's unchanged, the refitted ones (heat capacity, thermal conductivity,
  surface tension) were refitted over the wider range with their usual targets (0.21 %, 0.25 %, 0.20 %). The ideal-gas
  heat capacity record, refitted to the same values with other library versions, was kept as it was.
- **Not extended, refusal stays:** the liquid heat capacity below 289.69 K (so the liquid enthalpy, hence a flash, at
  15 degC). Its record carries `below_range_searched` (the list above), and the refusal prints it and says that a liquid
  at 289.69 K (16.5 degC) or above works. A feed at 16.5 degC or more works; **15 degC still does not**.
- Not changed: the vapour-pressure record. No open measured value below 290.26 K; ChemSep's equation, which reaches
  273.15 K, is 2.0 % lower at 288.15 K than the record, so the two differ by more than a rounding and choosing between
  them is a decision (below).
- The scipy reference of the liquid ethyl acetate esterification (`validation/fixtures/reactions.json`, regenerated by
  `reference_reactions.py`; only that entry kept, the vapour entries differ by library versions only) follows the new
  heat-capacity coefficients: dH at 320 K changed by 0.04 J/mol of 5391. The reference is computed from the records, so
  this is the data change reaching it, not a change made to pass a test.
- Full `npm test`: 1622 of 1623 pass after that, 1 known TODO.
- Test: `test/acetic-acid-supercooled.test.js`. Method of the test: NRTL (acid + water, METHOD_SELECTION.md).

## 4. Is the pure liquid's range the right limit in a mixture?

- METHOD_SELECTION.md rule 8 says "no temperature below the melting point of a component that is present (it would
  freeze)". For a dilute component that is wrong as written: acetic acid diluted in water does not freeze at 16.7 degC;
  the mixture's freezing is a solid-liquid equilibrium of the mixture (the Crystallizer has one), far lower.
- What the pure record range does in a mixture is give the **reference state** of the component's liquid enthalpy
  (the hypothetical pure supercooled liquid, with h^E from the activity model). That needs the pure liquid's heat
  capacity below its melting point, an extrapolation of a measured or databank curve into a metastable state.
- Other simulators: **not checked in this session** (the open documentation of DWSIM and The ChemSep Book was not read
  on this point). To do before the maintainer decides, as METHOD_SELECTION.md asks.
