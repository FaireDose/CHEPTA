# 2026-11-25-01: nitrogen inside the MHV1 rule; SRK + gE (MHV1) for CO2 + water; gas + liquid pair coverage

## Why

PR #264 (decision [2026-11-20-01](2026-11-20-01-co2-methanol-water.md)) showed that PR + gE (MHV1) follows the measured
CO2 and H2 solubility in methanol + water, where PR with k_ij overpredicts CO2 by about 77 %. The maintainer decided
(2026-11-25, relayed by the coordinating session): CHEPTA-Lab's methanol separator (Campos et al. 2022: methanol, water,
CO2, H2, CO, N2) and CHEPTA's methanol examples move to PR + gE (MHV1). But nitrogen had no MHV1 set, so the rule refused
Lab's stream. The maintainer: "we missed this". The pairs had been chosen on 2026-11-04 from Lab's first separator,
which had no N2 (decisions 2026-11-04-13 and [2026-11-06-02](2026-11-06-02-mhv1.md)).

This pull request: (1) N2 + water and (2) N2 + methanol sets inside the rule, (3) the state of SRK + gE (MHV1) for
CO2 + water, (4) a coverage table of every gas + liquid pair of Lab's development cases, and a script that prints it for
any list of components, (5) Lab's separator flash under PR + gE (MHV1) with N2.

Same script, rules, window (T ≤ 400 K, P ≤ 20 MPa), weights (each article the same total weight), NRTL form
(τ_ij = a_ij + b_ij/T, α = 0.3) and gate (AAD 5 % in P, every point with a bubble pressure) as decision 2026-11-06-02:
`validation/fit_mhv1_high_pressure.mjs`.

## 2. Nitrogen + methanol

**The search** (DATA_RULES.md, "How to search for a missing number", steps 2-5):

- ThermoML Archive: no N2 + methanol set (`validation/data/vle/archive_index.json`, the scan of the whole bulk file).
- OpenAlex (searches "nitrogen solubility methanol", "nitrogen methanol vapor-liquid equilibrium high pressure",
  "Henry constant nitrogen in methanol", and others) and CORE (`nitrogen AND methanol AND solubility`,
  `title:(nitrogen methanol)`, ...) found the measuring articles, all paywalled, with no free copy
  (`open_copy.py --all`, `--supplements`): Laursen and Andersen, J. Chem. Eng. Data 47 (2002) 1173,
  doi 10.1021/je020006z (DTU Orbit record, no file); Zeck and Knapp, Fluid Phase Equilib. (1986),
  doi 10.1016/0378-3812(86)80006-1 and 10.1016/0378-3812(86)85003-8; Weber, Zeck and Knapp, Fluid Phase Equilib. 18
  (1984), doi 10.1016/0378-3812(84)85011-6; Brunner et al., J. Chem. Thermodyn. 19 (1987), doi
  10.1016/0021-9614(87)90135-2; Fischer and Wilken, J. Chem. Thermodyn. 33 (2001), doi 10.1006/jcht.2001.0837 (298-398 K
  up to 10 MPa: the closest to the separator); Luehring and Schumpe, J. Chem. Eng. Data 34 (1989), doi
  10.1021/je00056a029. These are listed in the open copy of Malviya and Vrabec (2019), doi 10.1021/acs.jced.9b00571
  (TU Berlin DepositOnce, accepted manuscript), a Monte Carlo paper whose own values are simulations and not used.
- An open diploma thesis (M. Ogrizek, University of Maribor, 2011: N2 + methanol at 150-300 bar and 150-300 °C, CORE
  record 35915300) is outside the window and its PDF sits behind a robot check: for a person to download if wanted.
- A modelling paper (Archives of Thermodynamics 2019, doi 10.24425/ather.2019.129545) prints a PR k_ij of −0.163 for
  N2 + methanol: a model parameter, not data.

**Found open: IUPAC Solubility Data Series Vol. 10, Nitrogen and Air** (R. Battino, ed., Pergamon 1982;
https://iupac.github.io/SolubilityDataSeries/volumes/SDS-10.pdf, published open by IUPAC, the copyright holder, as
Vol. 5/6 already used for H2 + n-decane). Its compiled data sheets:

| Set (original article) | Sheet (PDF page) | Points | In the window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Krichevskii and Lebedeva, Zh. Fiz. Khim. 21 (1947) 715 | p 446 (491) | 21: 273-343 K, 4.9-29.5 MPa, liquid x | 16 (P ≤ 20 MPa) |
| Kretschmer, Nowakowska, Wiebe, Ind. Eng. Chem. 38 (1946) 506 | p 180 (225) | 4: 248-323 K, x at 1 atm partial pressure | 4 |
| Boyer and Bircher, J. Phys. Chem. 64 (1960) 1330 | p 181 (226) | 1: 298.15 K | 1 |
| Katayama and Nitta, J. Chem. Eng. Data 21 (1976) 194 | p 182 (227) | 5: 213-298 K, x at 1 atm partial pressure | 5 |

The evaluator (R. Battino, p 174, PDF p 219) rejected Just (1901) as too low and Makranczy et al. (1979) as too high,
and smoothed the other three to within 0.019 in ln x; those two are left out here for that reason. Boyer's sheet does
not say whether its x is at 1 atm partial or total pressure; it is taken as partial (the volume's convention, and it
agrees with the other two within 2 %), stated in its file. Files: `validation/data/eos/sds10_nitrogen_methanol_*.json`,
transcribed from the page images (110 dpi) and the PDF's text layer, which agree value by value. The 1 atm values go in
as the gas's partial pressure (`p_1_kPa` = 101.325), compared with y_N2 P of the bubble point, as the script already does
for such records. `fit_kij_high_pressure.mjs`'s `articles()` now also reads `sds<volume>_<gas>_<solvent>_*.json`.

**Fit** (`--only nitrogen+methanol`), within the gate, written without a warning:

| Model | Method | P: AAD / max % | solubility: AAD % | no bubble pressure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PR | **gE (MHV1)**: a = 4.3846, −0.3887; b = 10.44, 167.12 K | **2.8 / 8.4** | **2.7** | 0 |
| | ChemSep k_ij = −0.2141 (today's default of plain PR) | 24.8 / 38.5 | 32.9 (bias +31) | 0 |
| | constant k_ij fitted (−0.1794; not written) | 18.6 / 56 | 20.2 | 0 |
| SRK | **gE (MHV1)**: a = 4.3997, −0.2140; b = 13.02, 125.57 K | **3.7 / 8.8** | **3.6** | 0 |
| | ChemSep k_ij = −0.2881 | 22.3 / 36.1 | 28.8 (bias +27) | 0 |
| | constant k_ij fitted (−0.2571; not written) | 17.7 / 45.8 | 19.5 | 0 |

Per article (PR): Katayama 1.6 %, Kretschmer 1.8 %, Boyer 3.2 %, Krichevskii 3.5 % (bias −3.5 %). The articles' own
k_ij drift from −0.21 (213-298 K) to −0.14 (273-343 K): a constant k_ij cannot follow them, and ChemSep's k_ij
overpredicts the N2 solubility by about 30 %.

What MHV1 did before (proposal 0018, item 3): N2 + methanol has no NRTL set in the databank, so the rule refused the
pair ("a light gas with a polar liquid"). With the set it no longer does. No g^E = 0 fallback and no databank NRTL were
needed.

## 3. SRK + gE (MHV1) for CO2 + water

Not written (decision 2026-11-06-02, the maintainer's answer 3 of 2026-11-07): with α = 0.3 it was 16.0 % in P (6
points without a bubble pressure); with α fitted (0.1982) 14.0 % in P, 9.8 % in solubility, better than SRK's
k_ij = a + b/T (15.3 %, 11.1 %) but with 6 points without a bubble pressure against 4, so it failed the stated
criterion. Re-run today with the current engine: the same numbers (16.0 % and 14.0 % in P, 6 points without a bubble
pressure). It does not pass the gate (5 %), so it is still not written, and **SRK + gE (MHV1) still refuses any mixture
with CO2 + water**, Lab's separator included. Only PR + gE (MHV1) runs it.

(cloned read-only; all six are development cases, none held out):

**methanol-co2-campos-2022** (CO2, H2, CO, N2, methanol, water; O2 is 0 in every stream) and

**ethanol-zsm5-573k** (ethylene, water, ethanol, diethyl ether; a gas-phase reactor at 573 K):

| pair | PR k_ij | SRK k_ij | PR + gE (MHV1) | SRK + gE (MHV1) | NRTL | Henry |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**benzene-toluene-chemsep-b2, ethyl-acetate-piotrowski-2021, ethyl-acetate-sulgan-2020**: no gas.

So under **PR + gE (MHV1) every gas + liquid pair of the methanol cases now has a set fitted to open data**. The pairs
still without a set are the k_ij of H2, CO and N2 with water and H2 with methanol (plain PR/SRK use 0) and the ethylene
pairs.

## What was built

- Data: 3 ThermoML N2 + water files, 4 SDS Vol. 10 N2 + methanol files (`validation/data/eos/`); sources registered by
  `make_sources.py` (`src/data/sources.json`, `src/data/LICENSES.md`).
- `validation/python/hp_gas_solubility.py`: N2 + water added; `--only gas+solvent`.
- `validation/fit_kij_high_pressure.mjs`: `articles()` reads the SDS files too.
- `validation/fit_mhv1_high_pressure.mjs`: N2 + water and N2 + methanol in `PAIRS`; N2 + water in `HENRY_PAIRS`
  (recorded alternative); `HENRY_CHECK` and `check_henry`; N2 + water in `WARNED` as a proposal.
- `src/data/mhv1.json`: 19 sets (was 15); `src/data/known-issues.json`: the two N2 + water fit-quality issues (the
  script rewrites its MHV1 issues as a block, so they moved to the end of the file; their text is unchanged).
- `validation/python/reference_mhv1.py --write`: the independent references for the new fits
  (`validation/data/eos/mhv1_check.json`; `test/eos-mhv1.test.js` holds the engine to them).
- `validation/pair_coverage.mjs`: the coverage table.
- `test/mhv1-nitrogen.test.js`: the transcription, the gate and the comparison with ChemSep's k_ij (N2 + methanol), the
  per-article errors and the G7-04 check (N2 + water), the separator with N2, the coverage of the separator's pairs.
- `test/co2-methanol-water-solubility.test.js`: its assertion that MHV1 refuses N2 + methanol is replaced by the flash
  with N2 (2.2-2.6 % CO2): the refusal was the gap this work closes.
