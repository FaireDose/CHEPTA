## What was done

- **Two phases** (`src/equilibrium/reaction-equilibrium.js`, `phases: 2`): at each set of extents the mixture is
  flashed, and the activities come from the fugacities of the phases found; each reaction keeps its standard state, so
  vapour and liquid reactions can be mixed. The equilibrium reactor, the CSTR and the PFR take `phases`.
- **Gibbs mode** (`mode: "gibbs"`): the species given, a complete set of independent reactions written from their
  element matrix (a null-space basis), solved by the same solver (Rawlings and Ekerdt, section 3.5.2).
- **CSTR and PFR** (`src/units/kinetic-reactors.js`): the rate forms of the Reactions page; one phase or two; volume or
  catalyst mass; isothermal, a duty (0: adiabatic) or, for the PFR, a coolant; for the PFR a given pressure drop or the
  Ergun equation (Rawlings and Ekerdt eqn 7.69) with the gas viscosity given by the user. The PFR is integrated by the
  ode23s Rosenbrock pair (Shampine and Reichelt 1997, free copy from MathWorks; `src/util/ode.js`).
- **Your own kinetics** (`src/reactions/expression.js`): a formula of T, P, K, C, x, p, f, a and the user's constants,
  parsed without `eval`, with errors that name the position.
- **Views:**
  - one Reactor button with a menu of the five types;
  - the inputs of each type;
  - a kinetics editor on the Reactions page;
  - the profiles along a PFR under the drawing (select or double-click it), in place of the stream table, with a
    button back. The variables are molar flows, mole fractions, temperature, pressure, conversion, rates and vapour
    fraction against the length: the profile plots of desktop simulators (Aspen Plus's Plot Wizard on RPlug, DWSIM's
    PFR profiles).
- **The Reactor workspace** (Unit models; `src/ui/reactor-workspace.js`): a flowsheet of its own (feed, one reactor,
  product), edited with the Flowsheet's panels and solved by the same engine, saved in the project (`reactor`). Its
  curves:
  - the profiles;
  - the conversion, the outlet's mole fractions or the duty against the reactor temperature (isothermal at each);
  - the conversion against a CSTR's volume;
  - ΔrH and ln K of each reaction against temperature;
  - each rate against temperature at the feed's composition.
- **Tests:** `test/kinetic-reactors.test.js` against `validation/python/reference_kinetics.py` (scipy: the reactive
  flash, Radau for the PFR, fsolve for the CSTR, Robertson's problem), the Cantera fixture for Gibbs, Rawlings and
  Ekerdt Examples 4.4 and 4.2; `test/reactor-unit-workspace.test.js`.
  - Changed tests: three, each saying why. The navigation now has the Reactor workspace. The Unit models list it. The
    formula form is checked instead of refused as "a later step".
- Found on the way, not fixed here: the P-H flash with Peng–Robinson can fail on ethanol, water and diethyl ether when
  its temperature search passes through a two-liquid region (about 300 K): "no vapour-liquid split was found". A
  one-phase PFR does not use it (it solves its temperature from the phase's enthalpy).
