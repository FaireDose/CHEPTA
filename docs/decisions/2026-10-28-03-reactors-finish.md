## The search for biphenyl, p-terphenyl and cumene hydroperoxide

On 2026-10-28 the three components were added from the databases only (ChemSep, the WebBook, the ThermoML Archive);
the full-text step of the search order (docs/DATA_RULES.md, "How to search for a missing number", step 3) had not been
run. It is now: Europe PMC, OpenAlex and CORE, for every gap of the two components that have gaps (biphenyl has none),
recorded in `validation/data/searches/2026-10-28-fulltext.json` and the new list
[MISSING_PROPERTIES_2026-10-28-2.md](../MISSING_PROPERTIES_2026-10-28-2.md). Nothing open was found for the pure
compounds. Four documents that may hold values refused the scripts and are on the list to download by hand (the table
at the end of that file). No free supplement applies: no paywalled article with the numbers was found.

## Proposal 0010, steps 7 and 8

- Step 7 was done already (the Reactions page, export and import of reaction lists, the Excel export of every reactor);
  the proposal now says so.
- Step 8, the worked examples, in `test/kinetic-reactors.test.js` and listed in `docs/models/reactions.md` ("Worked
  examples"; `docs/BENCHMARKS.md` is generated by a script, so they are not added there):
  - **Ethanol to ethylene and diethyl ether over ZSM-5**, a plug flow with a rate per kg of catalyst, written as the
    user's own formulas: Aali and Hadi, Diyala J. Eng. Sci. 16(2) (2023) 30-49, doi:10.24237/djes.2023.16203, CC BY 4.0,
    open on the journal's site; eqn 4 (Table 1, p. 32) and eqn 14 (Table 4, p. 34), Table 9 at 573.15 K (k1 = 0.00868 and k2 = 0.00308
    mol/(g_cat h), K_A1 = 1.12604 and K_A2 = 7.50044 1/atm; p. 39) and Table 8 (K_eq1 = 361.8130, K_eq2 = 8.54626;
    p. 38). Pure ethanol at 1 atm, W/F = 1.166 g_cat h/mol, the article's largest. Checked against scipy (Radau,
    `validation/python/reference_kinetics.py`): outlet flows within 1e-5, rates at the inlet within 1e-9. The bed
    density (700 kg/m3) and the tube (1 cm) are assumed; with a rate per kg of catalyst they only place the bed.
  - **Phenol from cumene hydroperoxide in a CSTR**, Rawlings and Ekerdt Example 4.2 (p. 130-131): 85 °C, k = 4.12 1/h,
    Q_f = 26.9 m3/h, 37 m3. The book neglects the change of volume and finds 85 %; CHEPTA, with the outlet's volume from
    the liquid density records, finds 83.8 %: phenol and acetone take about 10 % more volume than the CHP fed. Pure CHP
    fed, at 5 bar (the book names no solvent and says the mixture is liquid), with the "ideal" model (no pairs or groups
    for CHP; a rate in concentrations reads the volume only).
  - Already in the tests and now listed: methanol from CO2 (equilibrium, Cantera), methanol from CO with its loop,
    esterification (equilibrium in one and two phases, scipy), Example 4.4.
  - Waiting for open data: benzene pyrolysis (Example 4.5; p-terphenyl's ideal-gas heat capacity), esterification
    kinetics (the TÜBİTAK article, to download by hand), HMF rehydration (levulinic acid's ideal-gas heat capacity).
- **The Ergun gas viscosity from the records:** left empty, the PFR takes the gas mixture's viscosity at each point
  from the components' low-pressure vapour viscosity records by Wilke's rule (J. Chem. Phys. 18 (1950) 517, as
  documented in the open `chemicals` library, `chemicals.viscosity.Wilke`); checked against that function. Outside a
  record's range it refuses and says to give the viscosity.
