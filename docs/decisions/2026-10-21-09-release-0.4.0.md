# 2026-10-21: version 0.4.0

The maintainer asked (2026-10-21): "after this and the exchanger stuff we need to release a new version on npm". Every
item of the roadmap's "v0.4 – Flowsheets" is done, so the version is 0.4.0 (package.json, package-lock.json, the CDN
links in the README, the examples and the AI instructions, the README's status and the roadmap).

What 0.4.0 adds since 0.3.0 (pull requests in FaireDose/CHEPTA):
- heat exchangers (proposal 0013): pre-sizing by zones along the real curves, U from Lienhard and Lienhard's tables,
  utilities, the wall thickness, the Heat exchanger workspace; energy streams, the heater's sizing and a two-stream
  block in the flowsheet; valve and pump; rating of a designed exchanger with its area and U kept;
- mixture entropy for every model, the P-S flash, compressor and expander; the Charts workspace (P-h, T-s and h-s
  charts with state points, the steam charts); the gas phase envelope through the critical point;
- neopentane, methylcyclohexane and helium; older ChemSep pairs imported;
- errors of the workbench as small warnings that open their details; the new logo; the website's cache fix and a title
  bar that never overlaps.

How it is published (the Publish workflow, `.github/workflows/publish.yml`): the maintainer publishes a GitHub release
with the tag `v0.4.0`; the workflow builds, tests and stages `chepta@0.4.0`; the maintainer approves it on npmjs.com with
two-factor authentication (chepta → Staged Packages).

The maintainer also confirmed the two choices of the rating (docs/decisions/2026-10-21-06-rating.md).
