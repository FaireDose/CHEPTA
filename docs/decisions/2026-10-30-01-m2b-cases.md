## What was done

- **Two liquids on the stages** (`phases: "VLL"`): `reference_mesh.py` gained stages with vapour and two liquids
  (`ActivityVLL`: each stage's liquid is its stable state, found by the tangent-plane test and a liquid-liquid split;
  K and the liquid enthalpy of the overall liquid, the averaging of The ChemSep Book, section 13.7.2, public domain)
  and a decanter on the condenser. Cases: **F1** now also with phases "VLL" (`expected_VLL`: solves, two liquids on
  stages 1 and 2; still PHASE_SPLIT with "VL"); **F2**, 1-butanol + water with a decanter (bottoms 0.994 butanol);
  **F3**, ethyl acetate + ethanol + water, two liquids on stages 5 to 10 only.
- **A bug found and fixed in the reference before it was used:** the first decanter version sent the condensate's
  overall composition to stage 2 instead of the reflux liquid's; its "solution" broke the overall butanol balance by
  half. The test now recomputes stage 2's balance with the decanter's reflux liquid.
- **Two solutions in F2:** besides the one in the fixture, a solution with two liquids on every stage and 0.46 butanol at
  the bottom closes every balance. The case names the reference's branch and its start; an engine that finds the other
  one reports it for review.
- **The column's own method** (**G1** to **G3**): benzene + toluene, the column in NRTL inside a Peng–Robinson
  flowsheet. The feed and the products keep their component flows, pressure and enthalpy flow across the boundary and
  find their temperature by a P-H flash in the receiving method. G1: the energy balance in the flowsheet's method closes
  to 1e-8 W (recalculating at the same T would have created 43.9 kW); a round trip returns within 1e-11 K. G2: the same
  with half of the bottoms recycled through a mixer in PR (converged in 37 passes, balance within 1e-6 W). G3: a PR
  liquid at 265 K that NRTL cannot represent (benzene's liquid records start at 278.68 K): refused, OUT_OF_RANGE.
- **Hydraulics for W4** (`validation/data/columns/hydraulics/`, `reference_hydraulics.py`): **H1**, the open worked
  example of Fair's method in Fermeglia's "Staged column design" (University of Trieste, 2021, slides 44-47): every
  printed number reproduced within 0.09 %; **H2**, a column whose slowest-flooding stage (the top) does not set the
  diameter, the bottom stage's larger vapour flow does (1.21 times), built so that the result holds for any fitted
  form of Fair's chart.
- Tests in `test/benchmark-columns.test.js`: the new cases are well formed; every reference closes its balances on
  every stage, recomputed with CHEPTA's own NRTL and Peng–Robinson (two-liquid stages, the decanter, the column's own
  method's feed); the liquids' equal activities and stability; the boundary's balance, round trip and refusal; the
  hydraulics recomputed. The engine's comparison is W2's and W4's.
- The source `fermeglia-staged-2021` now names slides 35-47 too; `make_sources.py` run.
