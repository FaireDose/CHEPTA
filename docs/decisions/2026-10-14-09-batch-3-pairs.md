# 2026-10-14: NRTL pairs for the batch 3 components of proposal 0009

- **Asked:** after batch 3c, the pairs of the new components, water + gamma-valerolactone (GVL) first ("start with
  one").
- **Data:** the whole NIST TRC ThermoML Archive (bulk file ThermoML.v2020-09-30.tgz, SHA-256 checked against the
  published one) was scanned again for the 140 components (`scan_archive.py`). 36 pairs of the 17 batch 3 components
  call for an activity model and have measured vapour-liquid data; `vle_batch.py` chose one set per pair by its rules
  (isobaric T-x-y near 101.3 kPa passing the point test first, then other pressures, isothermal P-x with the
  pure-component pressures, bubble temperatures alone), and `fit_parameters.py` fitted NRTL to it. UNIQUAC is not
  fitted: these components have no open UNIQUAC r and q.
- **Script fixes:**
  - `scan_pairs.py`: a component without a normal boiling point (`Tb_note`) is a liquid, not a gas; a pair with a
    component without critical constants takes an activity model even when both are nonpolar (an equation of state
    cannot be used).
  - `vle_batch.py`: a set whose temperature is a variable but stays within 0.5 K (a static apparatus held at one
    temperature, with the drift recorded per point) is read as one isotherm at the mean temperature. Before, each
    point became its own isotherm and the set was lost (Zaitseva et al. 2016, water + GVL at 350.2 K).
- **Written within the quality limits (18):** 1,3-propanediol with 1-hexanol, 2-propanol, ethanol, methanol,
  propylene glycol and water; GVL with ethanol, 2-propanol, methanol, formic acid and furfural; ethyl lactate with
  methanol, 1-propanol and dimethyl carbonate; furan with ethanol and n-hexane; cyclopentyl methyl ether + ethanol;
  gamma-butyrolactone + 1,4-butanediol.
- **Written with a quality warning and a "fit-quality" known issue (6):** 2-methylfuran + isooctane (2.37 % in P),
  2-propanol + furfuryl alcohol (1.96 %), furan + furfural (2.97 %), furan + toluene (2.44 %), ethyl lactate + methyl
  acetate (0.79 K), gamma-butyrolactone + tetrahydrofuran (0.63 K).
- **Not written, with the reason:**
  - water + GVL: the only set the rules accept is Zaitseva et al. 2016 (P-x at 350.2 K, both pure-component
    pressures measured). With every NRTL alpha tried (0.3, 0.2, 0.47) the best set predicts two liquid phases at
    350 K, and water and GVL are fully miscible, so it is not written (a fitted set never predicts a liquid split in
    its data range). Havasi et al. 2016 (bubble temperatures at three pressures) is not used: its pure GVL boils at
    480.2 K, 1.3 K from the vapour-pressure record (the rule asks 1 K), and it stops at 55 % GVL. Modified UNIFAC 2.0
    and the ideal solution remain for this pair, with the known deviation of Modified UNIFAC 2.0;
  - 1,3-propanediol + glycerol (5.6 % in P), 2-methylfuran + furfuryl alcohol (3.5 %), methyl lactate + water
    (3.1 %): beyond the wider limits;
  - no set meeting the rules: 1-butanol + 2,3-butanediol, 1-octanol + furan, 2,3-butanediol with ethyl acetate and
    with n-butyl acetate, water + gamma-butyrolactone (point test failed); ethyl acetate + ethyl lactate (pure boiling
    points 1.9 K from the records); water with levulinic acid and with ethyl levulinate (no pure-component pressures
    with the isotherm).
