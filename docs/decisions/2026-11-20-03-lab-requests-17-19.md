# 2026-11-20-03: CHEPTA-Lab's requests 17 and 19, and what was left of 8, 13, 14 and M2

CHEPTA-Lab checked CHEPTA main 415da41 on its two plants (ethyl acetate, Piotrowski & Kubica 2021; the methanol column
of Campos 2022) and asked for new changes (FaireDose/CHEPTA-Lab, main ca68c70:
cases/ethyl-acetate-piotrowski-2021/capability-requests-main-415da41/README.md and README.md, the table "On CHEPTA main
415da41"; cases/methanol-co2-campos-2022/CAPABILITY_REQUESTS.md). Release 0.6.3. Each fault was reproduced first on
this branch from Lab's own input; the inputs are copied unchanged into
`validation/data/columns/lab-requests-main-415da41/` and run by `test/lab-requests-063.test.js`.

## Request 19: a column purity that every column exceeds was refused with the wrong advice

**Reproduced.** Lab's R19 input (column 15 alone, 18 stages, R 2, 99.99 mass % water asked in the bottoms, a feed
nearly free of organics): `OUT_OF_RANGE`, "it stays between 0.999996 (at 18.07) and 1 - 2.3e-11 ... the reflux ratio
is too low ... a larger reflux_ratio, more stages". Every column was purer than asked, so the advice was the opposite of
what helps. Two causes: the scan of the distillate flow started at 1 % of the feed, and the refusal assumed the value
asked lay above the range.

**Fixed** (src/units/columns/specifications.js):

- where no scanned column crosses the value and the nearest column is the grid's first or last, the scan goes on toward
  that end of the range: a distillate of 0.3 % down to 1e-6 of the feed (0.997 up to 0.999999 at the other end), a ratio
  down to 0.001 or up to 990, each column from the last one solved, else from the cold start;
- a refusal whose value lies below every column reached says so: "Every column solved is purer than asked ... the
  specification is exceeded, not out of reach", with the advice reversed (a purer specification, a smaller reflux,
  fewer stages, or a flow specification), and, where the feed itself is already purer than asked, that a column that
  sends the other components to the other product cannot make this one less pure than its feed.

Lab's R19 is now refused with that message: its feed is 0.99996 mass fraction water, so no column takes its bottoms
down to 0.9999. In the plant (new-plant-dsinit-dist270, column 15 at loop iteration 38) the same refusal is given; it
says what the loop's state was, not that the column is too small.

A purity that a column does reach beyond the old grid is now solved: R19's feed with four times its organics (0.99984
water, a construction, not Lab's) is met at a distillate of 0.0325 mol/s, 2e-5 of the feed. It takes several minutes
(each cold column about 40 s; the warm starts fail at such small distillates), so it is not in the test suite.

## Request 17: the strict range mode inside a recycle loop

**Reproduced.** Lab's R17 input (the 15 degC feed F3 of acid and ethanol, a heater, a 50 % recycle, `strictRanges`,
2 iterations): `NO_CONVERGENCE` with nothing about the ranges; allowed to converge, `OUT_OF_RANGE`.

**Fixed** (src/flowsheet/flowsheet.js, src/runtime/worker.js), in two parts:

- a block outside every recycle loop is calculated once, from inlets that are already final (the blocks and loops
  before it are solved), so its records outside their ranges are those of the answer: in the strict mode it is refused
  at once, before the loops after it are solved, with the same message as at the end. Lab's R17 is now refused at F3 in
  a few milliseconds, whatever the loop does;
- a block inside a loop is still judged at convergence only. While a loop iterates its states are not the answer (a
  recycle may pass outside a record's range on its way and come back), and decision 2026-11-14-06 keeps "the answer's
  states, not the searches that led to it". What changes: a loop that stops without an answer (`NO_CONVERGENCE`, or a
  run stopped by its time limit, `OUT_OF_TIME`) now names the records its last pass used outside their ranges, and says
  that the strict mode will refuse the answer if they remain there (`details.extrapolations`; in the progress report,
  `strictOutside`).

What other simulators do was not read for this point; the choice follows CHEPTA's own rule of 2026-11-14 (judge the
answer). Question 1 below.

## Request 8, what was left: column 5 with 14 stages in the closed plant

**Reproduced.** Lab's plant62-R-bottoms-col5-14stages stopped in column 5 at loop iteration 5 (286 s). Column 5 alone
with the two inlets it had there (saved as `plant62-R-bottoms-col5-14stages-SCDS5-iteration5.request.json`): not
converged with 12 and 14 stages and two liquids allowed (phases "VLL"); converged with 8, 11 and 13 stages, and with
one liquid on each of 14 stages (phases "VL"), where no stage's liquid splits.

**Fixed** (src/units/columns/engine.js): a column with two liquids allowed that does not converge is solved with one
liquid on each stage; that column's stability check refuses any stage whose liquid would split, so where it converges
it is a solution of the two-liquid equations too, and their Newton is started from it. Column 5 with 14 stages now
converges at that iterate (60 s). Not for a decanter, a trial column of the specifications' outer loop, a step of a
continuation, or the smooth stage's ladder; where it does not help, the message says what the one-liquid column did.
With 12 stages the one-liquid column does not converge either: still open, not Lab's case.

## Request 13 and M2: a purity or a recovery inside the scanned range, not solved

**Reproduced.** Lab's M2 inputs on this branch before the change (main 415da41 gave the same): `NO_CONVERGENCE`, "lies
inside the range ... crossed between the distillate flow 1334 and 1373 mol/s, but the columns between could not be
solved".

**Fixed** (src/units/columns/specifications.js): inside a bracket, a trial column that cannot be reached from the
nearer end (nor by way of the points halfway) is solved once more from the cold start (the bubble-point start and the
continuation), before the bracket is given up. Both M2 inputs are now solved: the purity 99.985 mol % (186 s; bottoms
4581.6 kmol/h, the paper's stream 30 is 4581) and the recovery 0.99987 (405 s). Their tests are slow and run with
`CHEPTA_SLOW_TESTS=1`. Lab's request 13 purity in the plant (column 11 at 99.9 % ester, crossed between 63.9 and 84.9
mol/s) is the same case; it was not rerun here (16 minutes per plant run); Lab is asked to rerun it.

## Questions for the maintainer (recommendations first)

1. **Strict mode inside a loop.** Recommended: keep judging a loop's blocks at convergence (an iterate is not the
   answer) and name the last pass's records when the loop stops, as built; refuse at once only outside the loops. The
   alternative, refusing at the first pass that leaves a range, would refuse loops whose answer is inside every range.
   A middle way for later: refuse at once a block in a loop whose out-of-range state is fixed by its own
   specification (a heater given a temperature below a record), since no iteration changes it.
2. **The edge of the distillate's range.** The outer loop's Newton keeps the distillate between 1e-4 and 0.9999 of the
   feed (as before); only the scan goes to 1e-6. Recommended: keep it so.
3. **Time.** The cold starts that solve M2 and the request 19 variant cost 35-40 s each; M2 takes 3 and 7 minutes.
   Recommended: accept it for now (an answer instead of a refusal), and let the speed work of proposal 0027 look at
   the warm starts of the trial columns.
