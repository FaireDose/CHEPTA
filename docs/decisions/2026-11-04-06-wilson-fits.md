# 2026-11-04-06: Wilson fits to ThermoML data (proposal 0016, step 2)

The maintainer, 2026-11-04, relayed by the coordinating session: "next do also the ThermoML fitting so we are done with
this". This brings forward step 2 of proposal 0016, which the morning's answers (docs/decisions/2026-10-30-06-wilson.md)
had postponed until after M3. M1 and proposal 0016 are done with it.

## What was done

- `validation/python/fit_parameters.py` fits Wilson with the same data, objective and gates as NRTL and UNIQUAC
  (docs/DATA_RULES.md section 5): the generated fits of `vle_batch.py` (validation/data/vle/fits.json) and the hand-made
  FITS. `--models WILSON` fits and writes Wilson only; the NRTL and UNIQUAC sets and their fit-quality notes are left
  as they were.
- **Form:** b_ij and b_ji are fitted; a_ij = ln(V_j / V_i), a_ji = −a_ij, held at the liquid molar volumes at
  298.15 K from each component's density record. This is the constant-volume form of ChemSep's set (answer 1 of
  2026-11-04). For a component whose density record does not reach 298.15 K (methyl stearate, methyl palmitate,
  sulfolane, ethylene carbonate), the record's nearest temperature is used, as the engine does.
- **Not fitted with Wilson:**
  - liquid-liquid data and heterogeneous azeotropes (water + cyclohexane, ethylene glycol + cyclohexane, water + ethyl
    acetate);
  - pairs whose default NRTL set (else UNIQUAC) predicts two liquids inside the data's temperature range
    (1-butanol + water, methanol + n-heptane, furfural + water). A one-liquid fit to the bubble temperatures of a
    mixture that splits would hide the split.
- **Result:** 333 Wilson sets of 332 pairs written, tier "fitted". 332 are the pair's default; ChemSep's set stays as
  an alternative where the pair had one (44 pairs). One is a further, non-default set: water + ethanol fitted together
  with the excess enthalpies, as for NRTL and UNIQUAC.
  - 56 are within the wider limits only (2.5 K, 0.02, 3 %). Each has a quality warning and a "fit-quality" note,
    model WILSON.
  - 18 were not written because they fail the gates (furfural + water among them, which the two-liquid rule refuses as
    well), and 2 more because of the two-liquid rule. The run's output names each with its deviation.
- **Set names and source_ids** of the new sets are those of the NRTL or UNIQUAC set fitted to the same data. The
  pattern matching of `make_sources.py` had given some of them a wrong source: "Matsuda", an author of Kamihama et
  al., matched another article. The six sets with no NRTL or UNIQUAC counterpart have their sources from
  `make_sources.py`; they were checked by hand.
- **Ethanol + ethylene glycol:** the default Wilson set is now fitted to Kamihama et al. (2012), 0.07 K and 0.0006 in
  y. The known-issue note now says that ChemSep's set, still selectable, misses the data by 5.3 K.
- **Checks** (test/wilson.test.js, validation/fixtures/wilson.json):
  - The fitted defaults of six pairs against their open isobaric data are within 0.5 K and 0.012:
    - ethanol + water: 0.10 K
    - acetone + methanol: 0.12 K
    - acetone + chloroform: 0.15 K
    - chloroform + methanol: 0.17 K
    - ethyl acetate + ethanol: 0.09 K
    - ethanol + ethylene glycol: 0.07 K
  - The JavaScript engine equals the Python reference on both sets of each pair.

## For the maintainer to decide

Five pairs have a Wilson fit that passes the gates, while the NRTL and UNIQUAC fits to the same data were refused
because they predict a second liquid inside the data range:

| Pair | Wilson fit |
|---|---|
| 2-propanol + glycerol | 0.39 K |
| diethyl carbonate + water | 2.35 % in P |
| ethanol + n-decane | 1.00 % |
| o-xylene + sulfolane | 2.83 % |
| gamma-valerolactone + water | 0.85 % |

An NRTL split on VLE data is often spurious, which is why those fits are refused. Whether these mixtures really form
two liquids at the data's temperatures was not checked against open liquid-liquid data in this work, so they are kept
as written. Keep them, or refuse them until open miscibility data decide?
