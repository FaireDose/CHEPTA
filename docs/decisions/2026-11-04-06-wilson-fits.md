## What was done

- `validation/python/fit_parameters.py` fits Wilson with the same data, objective and gates as NRTL and UNIQUAC
  (docs/DATA_RULES.md section 5): the generated fits of `vle_batch.py` (validation/data/vle/fits.json) and the hand-made
  FITS. `--models WILSON` fits and writes Wilson only; the NRTL and UNIQUAC sets and their fit-quality notes are left
  as they were.
- **Form:** b_ij and b_ji are fitted; a_ij = ln(V_j / V_i), a_ji = −a_ij, held at the liquid molar volumes at
  298.15 K from each component's density record. This is the constant-volume form of ChemSep's set (answer 1 of
  2026-11-04). For a component whose density record does not reach 298.15 K (methyl stearate, methyl palmitate,
  sulfolane, ethylene carbonate), the record's nearest temperature is used, as the engine does.
- **Not fitted with Wilson:**
  - liquid-liquid data and heterogeneous azeotropes (water + cyclohexane, ethylene glycol + cyclohexane, water + ethyl
    acetate);
  - pairs whose default NRTL set (else UNIQUAC) predicts two liquids inside the data's temperature range
    (1-butanol + water, methanol + n-heptane, furfural + water). A one-liquid fit to the bubble temperatures of a
    mixture that splits would hide the split.
- **Result:** 330 Wilson sets of 329 pairs written, tier "fitted" (333 before the three removals for measured two liquids, below). 329 are the pair's default; ChemSep's set stays as
  an alternative where the pair had one (44 pairs). One is a further, non-default set: water + ethanol fitted together
  with the excess enthalpies, as for NRTL and UNIQUAC.
  - 54 are within the wider limits only (2.5 K, 0.02, 3 %). Each has a quality warning and a "fit-quality" note,
    model WILSON.
  - 18 were not written because they fail the gates (furfural + water among them, which the two-liquid rule refuses as
    well), and 2 more because of the two-liquid rule. The run's output names each with its deviation.
- **Set names and source_ids** of the new sets are those of the NRTL or UNIQUAC set fitted to the same data. The
  pattern matching of `make_sources.py` had given some of them a wrong source: "Matsuda", an author of Kamihama et
  al., matched another article. The six sets with no NRTL or UNIQUAC counterpart have their sources from
  `make_sources.py`; they were checked by hand.
- **Ethanol + ethylene glycol:** the default Wilson set is now fitted to Kamihama et al. (2012), 0.07 K and 0.0006 in
  y. The known-issue note now says that ChemSep's set, still selectable, misses the data by 5.3 K.
- **Checks** (test/wilson.test.js, validation/fixtures/wilson.json):
  - The fitted defaults of six pairs against their open isobaric data are within 0.5 K and 0.012:
    - ethanol + water: 0.10 K
    - acetone + methanol: 0.12 K
    - acetone + chloroform: 0.15 K
    - chloroform + methanol: 0.17 K
    - ethyl acetate + ethanol: 0.09 K
    - ethanol + ethylene glycol: 0.07 K
  - The JavaScript engine equals the Python reference on both sets of each pair.

## Two liquids in the extracted data (the maintainer, 2026-11-04)

The maintainer asked why the NRTL fits of the five pairs below were refused ("nrtl can predict two liquids"), how many
pairs are wrong on NRTL, and said: "the procedure should be fixed on how the liquid data is extracted".

- **Why:** `fit_parameters.check_phase_splits` expected one liquid unless a fit's data included a liquid-liquid file
  or a heterogeneous azeotrope. `vle_batch.py` read only the record sets with phases {Liquid, Gas}. ThermoML stores the
  two liquids of a three-phase point in their own sets ("Liquid mixture 1", "Liquid mixture 2"), so they were dropped,
  and the P-x or T-x rows at the two liquids' compositions were fitted as one liquid's bubble points.
- **How many:** of the 341 generated fits, 24 have no NRTL set written. 10 of those were refused only for a predicted
  split: 1-butanol + water, 2-propanol + glycerol, diethyl carbonate + water, dimethyl carbonate + glycerol,
  ethanol + n-decane, furfural + water, methanol + n-heptane, n-pentane + water, o-xylene + sulfolane and
  GVL + water. The 595 archive records the index lists for the 341 pairs were scanned for two-liquid sets:
  - 6 pairs have them with compositions: 1-butanol + water (2 records), acetonitrile + n-hexane, aniline + n-octane,
    diethyl carbonate + water, dimethyl carbonate + glycerol and furfural + water;
  - n-pentane + water has three-phase pressures without compositions, so nothing can be fitted from them;
  - the other 4 refused pairs have none.
  - One written set is wrong: acetonitrile + n-hexane's default NRTL set predicts one liquid at all six temperatures
    (313-348 K) where its own record measured two. It now has a "two-liquids-missed" note.
- **The fix:**
  - `vle_batch.py` reads the two-liquid sets, in the three layouts found in the archive, from every record of the
    pair. It writes them into the fitted data file as a `two_liquids` block, each row with its record
    (`--two-liquids` for the existing files). The six data files have it now.
  - `fit_parameters.py` fits these points as liquid-liquid equilibrium and leaves out rows strictly inside the
    measured split. It requires the split at those temperatures and no measured one-liquid row inside a predicted
    split.
  - An existing set that fails these checks never "stays" over a refit, and a refused refit takes out the set an
    earlier run fitted to the same data.
  - `make_sources.py` cites the records of a `two_liquids` block.
  - DATA_RULES section 5 says so.
- **The NRTL and UNIQUAC refits are deferred to D9** (the maintainer's choice). Refitting changes 1-butanol +
  water's default NRTL set, on which the column benchmark cases F1, F2 and W2 are built (they state ChemSep's set).
  The NRTL and UNIQUAC sets therefore stay as on main. A trial refit gave:
  - NRTL and UNIQUAC sets for 1-butanol + water, acetonitrile + n-hexane and furfural + water that predict the
    measured split;
  - none for diethyl carbonate + water (3.6 % in P; the record states about 10 % uncertainty on these pressures) or
    for dimethyl carbonate + glycerol (each fitted split covers measured one-liquid rows).
- **Wilson:** not fitted where two liquids are measured with the data. The earlier Wilson fits of acetonitrile +
  n-hexane, aniline + n-octane and diethyl carbonate + water are removed; the last was the maintainer's choice, over
  keeping all five.
