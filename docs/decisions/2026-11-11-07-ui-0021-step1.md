How it reads on the screen:

- **Marks.** Liquid compositions in the liquid colour and vapour compositions in the vapour colour, at the measured
  temperature (T-x-y) or pressure (P-x-y). A **filled dot**: the parameter set in use was fitted to this set. A
  **ring**: the fit compared its result with this set and did not fit it. A **cross**: the parameters in use were not
  fitted to it (a ChemSep set, Modified UNIFAC 2.0, an equation of state's k_ij, an ideal solution, or a fitted set
  whose record does not name the file). Hovering a point shows its values as measured, the citation and the table.
- **The list under the plot.** Each set drawn: the citation (a link to the open copy), the number of points and their
  pressure or temperature, the table, what the parameter set did with it and why, the deviation the fit recorded, and
  the deviation of the model on the screen (mean |ΔT| or |ΔP|, mean |Δy|), calculated at each measured point.
- **Sets at other conditions** are not drawn: they are listed with a button that draws the diagram there ("P-x-y at
  40 °C", "T-x-y at 50 kPa").
- **No data**: "No measured data: CHEPTA's data files hold no open vapour-liquid set of acetone + water. Nothing is
  drawn but the model." No point is ever calculated or filled in.
- **x-y** with the diagonal and the measured (x, y) points; **γ** of both components against x (a logarithmic axis
  when the values span more than a factor of 30); **α** of the first component to the second against x, with the line
  α = 1. Each at the diagram's pressure (from the T-x-y) or temperature (from the P-x-y), in mole fraction or wt %,
  with the readout in the Results panel. With an equation of state the γ plot is refused with the reason and what to
  do; x-y and α work.

## Choices made here (confirmed by the maintainer, 2026-11-12)

1. **The data are shipped inside the package, as one compact file.** `src/data/vle-points.json` is 348 kB; the browser
   bundle goes from 5.16 to 5.51 MB (+6.9 %; compressed for transfer 967 to 1062 kB, +95 kB). A file loaded on demand
   was not chosen: a chat artifact and a page opened from disk cannot fetch a second file, and those are CHEPTA's main
   cases. Only T, P, x and y of each row are kept; the citation is not repeated (it is in `sources.json` already).
2. **A set is drawn on a T-x-y when its pressure is within 0.5 % of the diagram's** (101.0 to 101.33 kPa on a diagram at
   101.325 kPa; 0.5 % in pressure moves a boiling temperature by about 0.15 K), and **on a P-x-y when its temperature is
   within 0.5 K** (the limit DATA_RULES.md, section 5, uses for "one isotherm"). The model's deviation is always
   calculated at each point's own measured pressure or temperature.
3. **Why a set was not fitted** is said in general terms ("a fit takes one set; the data rules choose it: isobaric
   T-x-y near 101.3 kPa that passes the consistency test first"), with the deviation the fit recorded. The reason for
   each single set is not stored anywhere a program can read; the set's `source` text sometimes has it ("which scatter
   more"). A structured reason per set would need a field written by `fit_parameters.py`.
4. **The role of a set is read from the parameter set's `source` text** (the file it names, and the files after
   "Check against"). It follows the set selected in the Inputs panel: with the ChemSep set selected, every point
   becomes a cross.
5. **x-y, γ and α are plots of the T-x-y and the P-x-y views** (a switch above the diagram), not three more buttons in
   the Diagrams group: they are the same calculation, and the group stays at five buttons on a phone.
6. **Relative volatility at the two ends** is read at a mole fraction of 0.000001 from the pure component (y/x is 0/0
   at the end itself). It is the ratio of the K-values of the engine's bubble point; nothing else is calculated in the
   view.
7. **Not saved in a project file**: which plot is shown and whether the points are on (the proposal: "which pane or tab
   is open is not saved"). The project format is unchanged.

- The canvas tabs of the proposal (step 2) are not built: the list of sets is under the plot, and moves to the Data

- UI_GUIDE.md is not edited (a change to the guide is a pull request of its own). It would gain, under "Charts": "a
  measured point is a small mark, filled where the parameters were fitted to its set, a ring where the set was only
  compared, a cross otherwise; its source is in its title and in the list under the chart".

## Seen while building, not changed

For methanol + water (NRTL, default set) the deviation recorded in the set's `source` for its two check files (AAD
0.81 K and 0.68 K in T) differs from what the engine gives today at the same points (0.67 K and 0.61 K); for the
fitted files of the pairs looked at (ethanol + water, water + acetic acid, methanol + water) the record and the
engine agree to the last digit. The check-file figures may predate a later change of the parameters or of a
vapour-pressure record. Both numbers are shown in the list, each named. Not investigated here.

## What the maintainer decided (2026-11-12, directly to the worker session: "i agree with yours")

All six recommendations of the table below are accepted: 1. the measured points stay inside the package; 2. a set is
drawn within 0.5 % of the pressure (T-x-y) or 0.5 K (P-x-y); 3. x-y, γ and α stay a switch above the diagram; 4. the
fitting script is to write a reason per data set (a follow-up); 5. the methanol + water check-file deviations are to
be looked into with the next data task; 6. no measured points on the γ and α plots for now.
