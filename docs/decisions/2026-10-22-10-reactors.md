## What was done

- **The conversion reactor** (`conversion-reactor`): each reaction converts a given fraction of a key reactant.
  - In parallel (the default) of the key's inlet flow; in series of what the reactions before it left.
  - Parallel conversions of one key above 1 are refused, and so is a co-reactant that runs out (named).
  - The outlet is at a given T, duty or vapour fraction, and P or pressure drop, flashed.
- **The yield reactor** (`yield-reactor`): products per kg (or mol) of key converted.
  - The yields must close the mass within 1e-6, with an optional closure component.
  - The element balance is reported in the notes, not enforced, as a lumped product has no exact formula.
- **The energy basis.** The stream enthalpies are against each component's ideal gas at 298.15 K (a nonvolatile
  component: its liquid). The heat of reaction is put on the same basis from the formation records
  (`formationReference`, `reactionHeat` in `src/reactions/reaction.js`), so duty = H_out − H_in + Σ ξ ΔrH_ref.
  - Where a component has no open enthalpy of formation, the reactor refuses and names it.
  - A heat of reaction the person gives with its source (`heatOfReaction`, on the reaction or on the yield reactor) is
    used instead, shifted from its standard state.
- **Balances.**
  - `runUnit` checks in + generation = out, and the elements (or the mass) of the generation, to 1e-9.
  - A flowsheet closes feeds + generation = products, and subtracts the heat of the reactions from its energy balance.
  - The flowsheet file takes a `reactions` list, and each reactor names the reactions it uses.
- **Tests** (`test/reactors.test.js`, 7 tests):
  - Methanol from CO₂ (Peng–Robinson at 1 kPa): the isothermal duty against Cantera's ΔrH at 350, 550 and 700 K,
    within 1e-4.
  - An adiabatic reactor, then the isothermal one at its outlet temperature, which needs no duty.
  - Parallel and series reactions, and the cases that are refused.
  - The yield reactor on esterification (ideal solution), with a lumped product and a given heat of reaction for
    furfural to 2-methylfuran.
  - A methanol loop with a 5 % purge against the hand calculation, with the overall balances.
  - The heats of reaction "made up for the test" are labelled so in the test; they are not data.

## Found on the way

- **Levulinic acid** melts above 298.15 K, so its liquid enthalpy is anchored where its records start. That needs its
  ideal-gas heat capacity, which has no open data. So no stream with levulinic acid has an enthalpy, and HMF
  rehydration (step 8) waits. The test uses esterification instead.
- **A flash with hydrogen and the "ideal" model** at 550 K does not finish (on main too). The reactor tests use
  Peng–Robinson, which METHOD_SELECTION.md asks for with gases above their critical point. To look into.
