# 2026-10-21: errors as small warnings

The maintainer (2026-10-21): "you need to clean a bit the errors on the workbench. make every error just a small
warning and the user needs to press on it to see more details".

Every error and problem box of the workbench is now one small warning (`src/ui/warning.js`):
- closed, it is one line with a warning sign and the message's first sentence, at most 64 characters;
- pressed (or Enter / Space), it opens the whole message and its details: the list of problems, a hint, a button.

It is a native `<details>` element, so it works with the keyboard and screen readers without script. A message of one
short sentence is shown whole, without a Details link.

It covers:
- the canvas when the inputs are incomplete (it was a large box) and when a calculation stops;
- the problems under the inputs (several problems: "3 problems with these inputs: …");
- the known deviations of a model, the notes of a diagram (points not drawn, a liquid split), the flowsheet's "Not
  solved", the My data panel's errors, the property calculator and the simple widget (`CHEPTA.mount`).

The messages themselves are unchanged.
