The coordinating session's task: proposal 0015 steps 5 and 8, liquid-liquid data with joint fits, then the Extraction

and the fits. The Extraction block and its ternary view come in a second pull request

## What main had

The two-liquid flash, the stability test, and binary liquid-liquid fits (water + ethyl acetate jointly with its
infinite-dilution and azeotrope data; water + cyclohexane; ethylene glycol + cyclohexane). There were no ternary
tie lines and no fit to a ternary.

## The system: water + acetic acid + ethyl acetate

This is the textbook extraction of acetic acid from water by ethyl acetate. All three pairs are in CHEPTA, fitted to
open binary data. Found in the NIST TRC ThermoML Archive (open data; the articles are subscription-only):

- Roque, Pinto, de Oliveira, Rabelo, Fluid Phase Equilib. 463 (2018) 34: 12 tie lines at 293.2 and 313.2 K, stated
  uncertainty 0.012-0.016 in mole fraction (validation/data/lle/water_acetic-acid_ethyl-acetate_lle_roque2018.json).
- Toikka et al., Fluid Phase Equilib. 373 (2014) 72: 14 tie lines at 303.15 and 313.15 K, stated uncertainty
  0.010-0.019 (..._toikka2014.json).

**With the default sets (binary data only), the model misses the tie lines** by up to 0.16 (NRTL) and 0.22
(UNIQUAC) in the ester-rich liquid. That is too far off for an extraction calculation.

## The joint fit (validation/python/fit_ternary_lle.py)

- The three pairs are fitted together, each temperature-dependent. The objective: the tie lines of Roque 2018, each
  measured mole fraction weighted by its stated uncertainty (the maintainer's target, 2026-10-29), plus each pair's
  own binary data with the files and weights of its default fit.
- The result is a further, **non-default** set of each pair, `fitted-lle-roque2018`. The three are chosen together
  (`sets`). The default sets do not change.
- **Toikka 2014 is a check, not fitted.** Its two tie lines without acid (the binary edge) put 0.142 and 0.162 water
  in the ester-rich liquid at 303.15 and 313.15 K. The binary data CHEPTA fits (Grande and Marschoff 2005) give 0.1885
  and 0.2111, and Roque 2018 agrees with Grande. The sets disagree, and the binary data decide (DATA_RULES section 3).

| Mean absolute deviation (max) | NRTL | UNIQUAC |
|---|---|---|
| Roque 2018, acetic acid, water-rich / ester-rich liquid | 0.0060 / 0.0074 (0.013 / 0.017) | 0.0102 / 0.0080 |
| Roque 2018, water in the ester-rich liquid | 0.021 (0.059) | 0.039 (0.106) |
| Toikka 2014 (check), acetic acid | 0.0062 / 0.0089 | 0.0079 / 0.0057 |
| Toikka 2014 (check), water in the ester-rich liquid | 0.063 (0.19) | 0.077 (0.25) |
| water + acetic acid, 1-atm T-x-y (Chang 2005) | 0.28 K, 0.0088 in y (default 0.24 K, 0.0085) | 0.19 K, 0.0073 |
| ethyl acetate + acetic acid, P-x at 323 K (Brandt 2014) | 0.13 % (default 0.12 %) | 0.32 % |
| water + ethyl acetate, heterogeneous azeotrope at 1 atm | 70.90 degC (default 70.61; handbook 70.40) | 71.29 degC (default 70.76) |

For the solute, acetic acid, the NRTL set is within the stated uncertainty. NRTL is the better of the two for this
system.
